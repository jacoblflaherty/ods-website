Ottawa extends post-graduation work permits

Published March 29, 2023 · OnDemand Staffing

“We need to use every tool in our toolbox to support employers who continue to face challenges in hiring the workers they need to grow.”

Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada announced measures letting international graduates with expiring post-graduation work permits extend their status and keep working in Canada. For Niagara employers facing hiring challenges, it keeps skilled, job-ready candidates in the local labour pool.
